# NOT

NOT is the odd one out: it only takes a single input, and it simply flips
it.

```
NOT 0 = 1
NOT 1 = 0
```

Where AND/OR/XOR combine two switches into one answer, NOT just inverts
one switch by itself. It's the operator you reach for whenever you want
the opposite of a condition instead of a combination of two.

## Real world

- Inverting a condition ("if NOT logged in, show the sign-in screen")
- Bitmasking to compute a complement
- Hardware inverters, physically etched into a circuit
