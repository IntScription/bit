# Bits

A bit is a single switch. It is either **off (0)** or **on (1)** — nothing
in between.

Everything a computer stores or computes is ultimately made of bits: a
transistor either lets current through or it doesn't, a magnetic domain
points one way or the other, a pit on a disc reflects light or it
doesn't. The bit is the physical fact underneath all of it.

## Real world

- Transistor states inside a CPU
- Magnetic domains on a hard drive
- Pits and lands on an optical disc
